How the workbench works

Build momentum without starting over.

  1. Choose the next project stage: define the smallest useful outcome, its cost and who benefits.
  2. Build the funding stack: keep every credible grant, fundraiser, sponsor and in-kind layer visible.
  3. Confirm people and authority: identify the legal applicant, permissions, roles and decision pathways.
  4. Prepare the evidence once: maintain profiles, quotes, budgets, risks, milestones and public-benefit proof.
  5. Apply, fundraise, deliver and report: record what was promised and collect evidence as the work happens.
