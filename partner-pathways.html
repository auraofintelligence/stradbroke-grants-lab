Potential connection pathways

Work out the role before asking anyone.

This desk connects project needs with sourced research leads. A possible connection is not a confirmed partner, applicant, supporter or authority.

Roles stay distinct

Lead applicant, auspice, cultural authority, site or asset controller, adviser, delivery organisation, supplier, sponsor, supporter and beneficiary are different roles. Each needs its own evidence, decision pathway and conversation.

Read the status before the name

Research lead
Found in a public source; not contacted.
Conversation only
A discussion occurred; no role or support is agreed.
Interested
Interest is recorded; authority and terms still need confirmation.
Authorised
The right decision-maker has confirmed a defined role and evidence is recorded.
