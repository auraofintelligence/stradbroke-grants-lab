01 / Start on the island

Map it. Share it. Keep it available.

Picture an island digital twin that helps people understand a place or rehearse a scenario. A local noticeboard could publish useful updates from that work. A disaster-ready kiosk could keep approved information available if connectivity drops. These are connected proposals for Minjerribah, with different people, permissions and evidence needed at each step.

02 / Follow a public decision

From a council question to national ideas

Start with the decision, who has authority and what evidence is public. P4A’s civic workbench connects local questions to state and national proposals. Luke’s Relevance shows how one person worked through the laws that affect their life.

03 / Build something together

From one contribution to shared infrastructure

C-Hour explores how to recognise time people choose to give. Mutual Futures explores shared ownership and care. The Ready S.E.T. Cultural Intelligence Node is the updated version of the Trust Hub. It develops the earlier local-space concept through the design of a single computing node. Installing the Missing Middle asks what a proposed network of 3,000 nodes would take.
