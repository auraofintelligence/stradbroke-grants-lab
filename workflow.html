How it works

Build funded momentum without starting over.

Prepare the truth once, fund the next useful stage and keep the evidence ready for every later layer.

  1. Choose the next useful stage: define the outcome, place, beneficiaries, rough cost, timing and the people with authority.
  2. Shape a funding stack: match grants with possible resident or member fundraising, sponsors, visitor giving, philanthropy, earned income and in-kind contributions.
  3. Keep every useful amount: a small grant may pay for planning, permissions, a quote, a pilot, evidence, program delivery or equipment without covering the whole project.
  4. Confirm the applicant and roles: test legal entity, ABN and GST needs, auspice, cultural authority, site control, delivery responsibility and decision pathways.
  5. Prepare reusable evidence: maintain public benefit, demand, profiles, budgets, quotes, permissions, risks, milestones and reporting duties.
  6. Draft for the exact doorway: answer the funder's questions or prepare the fundraiser and supporter message without over-claiming.
  7. Review and authorise: check eligibility, facts, attachments, conflicts, cashflow, privacy and who can approve submission or outreach.
  8. Deliver and collect proof: record spending, decisions, participation, outputs, changes and evidence as the work happens.
  9. Report, acquit and stack again: close the obligations, show what the layer unlocked and prepare the next stage.
